# PersonaFoil Roadmap

## v0.1.x

- Complete controlled outgoing-UID hardware validation
- Complete real release-to-release updater validation
- Compatibility fixes and low-risk UX polish
- Better diagnostics and translations
- Real-hardware screenshots and distribution metadata

## v0.2.x candidates

- Richer management of PersonaFoil-generated personas
- Carefully designed export/import of PersonaFoil-generated personas only
- Better update UX and recovery diagnostics
- Accessibility and localization improvements

## Research

- Application interoperability improvements that preserve the local, reversible identity model

## Explicitly not promised

PersonaFoil does not promise global Switch identity replacement, certificate impersonation, PRODINFO editing, or third-party restriction-bypass features.
